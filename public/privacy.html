Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

When you submit an enquiry form we collect your name, mobile number, email address and the apartment type you are interested in. We also record which form you used and, if you arrived from an advertisement, the campaign tags in the link (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign).

Why we collect it

Only to respond to your enquiry: to call you, message you on WhatsApp or email you the brochure and site visit details for Eldeco La Vida Bella. We do not sell your details.

Who can see it

Our sales team through a password-protected system. We may use service providers (such as hosting, WhatsApp and email) strictly to run this enquiry process. Where required by law, we may share information with authorities.

How long we keep it

For as long as needed to follow up on your enquiry, and no longer than 24 months unless you become a customer.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your details, or to stop contacting you, at any time. Call or WhatsApp +91 78603 91489 and we will act on your request. You may also withdraw consent by replying STOP to any message.

Cookies

This page does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The team login uses one strictly necessary session cookie.
