Eldeco La Vida Bella is a gated community in Sector 12, Greater Noida West (Noida Extension), offering 3 BHK and 4 BHK condominiums. Five G+22 towers share 4 acres, of which 1.70 acres is landscaped greens, and the 35,000 sq. ft. G+2 clubhouse sits at the centre of the community.
